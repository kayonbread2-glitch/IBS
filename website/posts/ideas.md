# Psychological slow burn ideas
This page contains bullet pointed ideas or premises not fixed or planned writing.

## The Neighbor

Neighbor has the same routine every day. Of what I can see:
- Wake up and water the plants
- Cut the grass
- Water the plants again
- Check the main
- The plants are dead? water them again.

Recently though he has started to stray from this fixed state. Last night just
before his final plant watering he paused. He turned only his eyes to look at
me first. I was sitting in a chair on my patio reading as I usually do so I
might not have noticed immediately. Soon he turned his whole head to face me.

He stood frozen there facing the exact same direction for almost 3 hours.

___

## The Guest in the Glass

Entity only visible through glass or in reflections. Pictures reveal entity is
truly standing next to narrator. Looking at entity is always associated with
heavy visual distortion and migraines.

___

## Stolen reflection

Reflection starts showing signs of delay. As if someone is attempting to copy
their movements rather than actually being a copy. 

- maybe camera shows a lack of reflection or the reflection simply stares at the camera on film

___

## Ritualistic Withdrawal

Foreign entity somehow communicates with host providing initially harmless life
advice that spirals into unreasonable requests. Non compliance spawns worsening
symptons than ever before.

Start with better sleep every night, 8 hours.

Then something seemingly pointless like turning off a specific breaker or
flicking switches a fixed number of times.

Move forward into off the rails requests of violence or harm. The victim should
refuse until the pain becomes unbearable. Perhaps a voice in their head is the
communication and pain is extreme migraines that builds into internal bleeding
and other life threatening conditions.

Slow realization that most of these steps are partaking in a demonic ritual of some form.

___

## Predictive motion sensor

Motion sensor starts writing logs for future dates

ex: [09-06-2027] [01:00:00 - 01:05:00] Triggered 3 times

logs happen to line up exactly with the future. Writes a log where there is an
unreasonably amount of actiivity at a time where the narrator would not be home
or active.

___

## Can't hear

Deaf narrator uses speech to text app to communicate. App starts generating
nonsense while alone.

## Spatious house echo

Echo transforms into an almost mocking like tone slowly driving the narrator
mad. Obsessed wit hthe idea that something is there. Perhaps driven to tear
apart living space to find nothing.

___

## Alien hand syndrome

Fae like entity replacing instead of people just parts of them. Maybe ones
vocal cords or a hand. Maybe the victim never even notices, but others do.

___

## Contagion

Perceived traumatic events that exist only as hallucinations can be passed to
others similar to a mass psychosis. Simply talking of these events causes
others to experience these problems in addition.
