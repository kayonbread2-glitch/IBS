Hey guys, you all offered me some great advice to work on memory problems and I
have some news. Some of it is good and some bad. We'll start with the good.

I bought some new mugs and I have been capable of successfully pouring my
morning coffee and actually drinking. I know right? It's truly crazy. At least
for me its quite nice to be able to start my day well again.

My memory issues have been progressing in other places since the coffee
incidents though I'm feeling optimistic about applying the tools I've learned to
these new challenges. I've started forgetting things like setting my clothes out
at night to change into in the morning or forgetting to change into my night clothes.

I'm trying to remain positive about this whole situation and just see it as a
new challenge that I have to overcome. A natural progression of my life and
doing my best to deal with the cards I've been dealt.

Now for the bad news. The coffee situation is worse than I originally thought.

last post, well, many of them were gifts from the people around me.
