# The Reset Button

Hey, welcome to my first blog post of hopefully many. Recently I've made the
decision to start taking control of my life. Take action towards my own self
betterment. 

This blog will only focus really on 1 of the aspects of the many things I'd like to try,
but hopefully I can impact someone else positively. Maybe there is just 1
person who can learn from me... Or! Maybe I become a pro at this and can start
offering advice!

Regardless let me get down to the reason why I made this site. I've been having problems
with my anxiety, sleep, etc. I'm hoping that a type of meditation I found
online will help me reduce these interferences in my life.

Earlier this week I was doing some research on tools to help me manage my
symptoms when I came across an old blog post from 2009. It was talking of the
human ability to unlock a "guiding voice" through daily mindfulness and
meditative practices. After reading about it, it seemed very simple and
honestly anything is worth a try.

If I don't have any progress with this method then I will look for the next
one! I'll keep you guys updated (If anyone actually reads this in the first
place)

# Improvement

Well! It's time for the first update!

It makes me happy to see how many of you tuned in to read about the beginning
of my self improvement journey! I can only hope my story provides as much
benefit to all of you as your support provides for me.

I'll start by talking about what this method is in the first place since I
completely forgot to in my initial post.

Basically it is a few very specific modifications to a typical meditative practice.

The first modification is the position. You must sit on the floor with your
*right* foot on your *left* knee, and your *left* foot stretched out straight. The
original post is very specific about this only working in that direction so I
don't recommend swapping your feet.

Your back should be as upright as you can manage though as I'm not too flexible
currently I have to lean slightly back against the wall at an angle.

Your hands must laid flat on the correspending thigh.

The second adjustment is the mantra. You must speak the phrase
