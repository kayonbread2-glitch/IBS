# <abberated-text amplitude="0.1">Erasure</abberated-text> [Next](?q=erasure-update1)

I don't know where else to post this but let me start with just my question. Is
this how it feels to forget?

Where do I start? 

I feel like I'm losing my mind. For weeks now I've been forgetting stuff
*constantly*. Ive scheduled a vist with a neurologist for next week to see if
they can tell me anything about my sudden forgetfulness.

The first thing I can remember that was unusual for me was just a mug. 

I made coffee, poured myself a mug, and carried it into the living room. when I
sat at my desk to do some work on my laptop, I noticed. The mug was cold. I look
over to see an empty cup in my hand. It was spotless. There wasn't ever any
coffee in it.

The coffee machine was empty too. It was exactly as I left it from the day
before. The coffee I had just bought still had the factory seal on it. I never
made coffee. I just walked into the living room with an empty cup? That's not
like me. 

This coffee problem has been progressing day to day since then. Just yesterday
when I performed the same routine? No mug at all. I tried to reach for my mug
and in its placed my hand only registered the air.

Did I ever even own any mugs? I don't even know anymore. My cabinet that was
full of mugs? Different ones Ive collected from vacations with family and some
that have been gifted to me? All gone. 

Every mug I have ever owned. I thought someone must have broken
in while I was asleep last night! What else am I to think but no. The police
came. They said there was no sign of break-in. And also? Who breaks into an
apartment to steal a collection of cheap personalized cups and *nothing else*.

I'm starting to seriously worry for my sanity. How did I manage to throw
away so many mugs and not remember. Why did I do that? Why can't I remember
anything anymore. I hope someone can offer me some advice before it can get any
worse.

I can't live like this.
