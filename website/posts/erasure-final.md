# <abberated-text amplitude="0.6">Erasure - Final</abberated-text> [Previous](?q=erasure-update1)

I've decided this will be the final update. There is nothing any of you can do
for me anymore. I appreciate all of the help and kind words I have received from
those of you reading but I am now truly hopeless.

Just a couple weeks ago the worst thing in my life was forgetting my morning cup
of coffee. This week? well. Let me tell you.

Same morning routine. Wake up. Get dressed. Oh wait? wheres my clothes? Didn't
set them out again.

Make my coffee. Pour my cup. In my plain white mugs I bought because apparently
I used to use blankets to drink my morning coffee. Im b'm glad that now I'm a
logical person using a liquid storage device to drink from instead of a pile of
intertwined threads.

Sit down in my living room. Place my cup on the right side of my laptop on the
coaster. 

I go to reach for it. Nothing, just air again. Obviously I walked into the
living room with no cup once again right? no. no. no. no. no. NO!

<shake-text>NO</shake-text>

Of fucking course not.

I looked over and saw the steam rising out of the dark black liquid filling the
cup. Something was missing though. My fucking hand. I don't have a right
hand anymore. Never did.

I've never had a right hand. My mother insists it's a birth defect. It's missing
in every photo I can find. I can even write with my left now. As if I've been
doing it my whole life! I mean how else did I get through school with just that
hand right?

I. Don't. Know. What. Is. Happening. 

I'm being erased. Everything I've done. Everything I am is just slowly being erased.

It starts with the coffee. I poured it? No I didn't.

I set my clothes out? No I didn't.

I worked on my laptop? NOPE!

<abberated-text amplitude="0.6" speed="0.015">I am missing a hand... </abberated-text>

What have I accomplished? apparently nothing.

Who am I? apprently no one.

I am dwindling away. How long before I lose my left hand? Or an arm? I don't
know but it feels inevitable. Everyone thinks that I'm just going crazy but the
worst part is that it's true but not in the way they seem to think.

Maybe they are right. Maybe this is how I always was.
