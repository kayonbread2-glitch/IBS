# Artifact of Kay - Specimen #006

**Classification:** No Threat - Perceptual Anomaly / Uncontained

**Protocol:** 
> Direct containment is impossible but yet unnecessary. Specimen 
#006 is uniquely benign in that it is incapable of interacting with the world 
to our understanding. Researchers should consider cognitive shielding when 
working in areas of known infestation to prevent episodes of paranoia.

> If you are currently experiencing a manifestation,

> <shake-text>Do not attempt to look</shake-text>

> Keep your gaze fixed on the task at hand. Do not panic. Specimen #006 can 
not hurt you.

> We do not know what Specimen #006 seeks to gain from its observations. Yet it 
seems not to rest. While only appearing to one individual at a time, it also 
seems to *always* appear to one individual.

**General:** 
> Specimen #006 is a scrawny human-like figure that does not 
seem to understand walking. It fumbles like a toddler ever attempting to right 
itself upon its feet, yet always focused upon its task. 

> <abberated-text amplitude="4.0" speed="0.015"> Specimen #006 will never look away.</abberated-text>

> Specimen #006 appears to be in a constant state of obsession with human existence. 
It is always studying our behaviors, our habits. We theorize that this profiling is 
highly detailed and perhaps being collected as part of some unknown goal. There is
no telling the horrors we'll experience if completed.

> Additionally, if you come to note that this specimen has looked away, if you even 
think that it might be the case:

> <shake-text amplitude="3.0">run</shake-text>

> What you are seeing is **NOT** Specimen #006.

> Specimen #006 seems to behave in a progressive manner we have devised into stages:

>> **Stage 1**: Specimen #006 appears only for momentary flickers in the cusp of the 
hosts vision. Often times pre-emptive fear causes a misreporting of stage 1 encounters 
with Specimen #006. Do not report stage 1 encounters.

>> **Stage 2**: Specimen #006 sharpens as if being draw into view by some foreign hand. 
Note that specimen #006 will never appear fully "Drawn in". Specimen #006 typically begins 
to "walk" in this stage... Or at least it attempts to produce such animation.

>> **Stage 3**: Only few will have stage 3 encounters with Specimen #006. The specimen at
this stage of its observation begins to reach inwards as if trying to reach into the hosts 
reality. attempts as of yet are unsuccessful and we hope they stay as such. 

> <shake-text amplitude="1.0">Report stage 3 encounters immediately</shake-text>
